<objective>
Fix the two fire-model findings from 02-REVIEW.md that corrupt every deterministic number downstream:
CR-01 (the CA kernel reads Open-Meteo's meteorological FROM wind direction as a TOWARD direction, so fire spreads upwind) and
HI-02 (spread direction and rate measure the P50 centroid shift plus grid-snapping error instead of front advance, and divide by a hard-coded 3 hours).

Purpose: D-03 says wind direction and speed from the Weather agent adjust the spread kernel. D-05 says FireSpreadResult reports spread_direction_deg and spread_rate_m_per_h. Today both are wrong. These values feed the P50/P90 polygons, the route penalties (02-05, 02-07) and the LLM rationale (02-06).
Output: corrected ca_model.py and ensemble.py, a nullable spread_direction_deg in FireSpreadResult, and regression tests that fail on the old code.
</objective>
