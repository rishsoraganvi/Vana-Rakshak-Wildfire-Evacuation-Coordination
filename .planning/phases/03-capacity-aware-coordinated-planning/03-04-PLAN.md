<objective>
Add the Phase 3 persistence schema (migration 0006), the labelled synthetic seed data with validated loaders and idempotent seed scripts, and the Shelter agent registry. The registry covers usable capacity, perimeter exclusion, candidate listing and the atomic `update_occupancy` compare-and-set.

Purpose: PLAN-04 needs a shelter registry with conservative usable capacity, and capacity writes that cannot overbook. PLAN-08 needs plan_versions columns for parent link, validation, freshness and provenance, a `failed` status and DB-level immutability.
