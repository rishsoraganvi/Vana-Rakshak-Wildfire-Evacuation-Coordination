  <behavior>
    - Capacity: for every shelter, the sum of its legs is at most `max(0, available[s])`
    - Conservation: for every group, the sum of its legs plus `unassigned.get(g, 0)` equals its persons
    - Banned and None-cost pairs never appear in legs
    - `per_pair_cap[g] = c` limits every leg of g to at most c persons, which forces a split when c is below persons
    - Split: one group of 300 against shelters with 200 and 150 available gives two legs that sum to 300
    - Unplaceable: a group whose costs are all None is unassigned with reason `no_reachable_shelter`. A group short of capacity gets reason `insufficient_capacity` and its residual persons.
    - Determinism: two calls with the same inputs return equal MatchResult objects
    - When total available is at least total persons and every pair is finite, unassigned is empty
  </behavior>
