<objective>
Upgrade the Routing agent (TechSpec §7.3) for PLAN-03. This adds ETA-aware fire penalties per forecast horizon (D-07), honours road edge status, builds a settlement-to-shelter cost matrix, finds lazy k=3 shortest paths on a collapsed DiGraph, adds the BPR congestion model (D-08), and makes graph loading load-only with an explicit build script (D-05, D-06, D-19).

Purpose: Routes must account for blocked and unsafe edges, fire forecast penalties and changing road conditions, without downloading the whole AOI at plan time (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).
Output: `costs.py` horizon attributes, `router.py` matrix/snap/evaluation, `k_paths.py`, `congestion.py`, load-only `graph.py`, `scripts/build_osm_graph.py`, and tests on the synthetic graph.
</objective>
